Bulk selection

The Archived view with two rows checked. The bar appears with the first checked row and counts them without any script; Clear unchecks them all, and the header box selects every row on the page. In the Archived view the bar offers Restore and Delete permanently; in Active, Archive; in All, Archive and Restore.
