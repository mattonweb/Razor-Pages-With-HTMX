Inputs

Entering and changing values. Controls take no class: write them with the tag helpers and the brand styles what they emit. The styles are in assets/css/inputs.css, over the control defaults in assets/css/base.css.

Field

A .field holds the label, the control, an optional .hint, then the validation message. A required field gets its asterisk from aria-required="true" or the data-val-required the tag helpers add. An invalid control carries .input-validation-error and aria-invalid="true", and points at its message with aria-describedby. An error that belongs to no single field, such as a save that someone else's save overtook, goes in the validation summary at the top of the form.

Rows

Several values of one kind that the user writes, such as telephone numbers: a row for each, its control and its Remove button, and Add after the rows. Every row's control has the same name. Remove shows only when there is more than one row, and Add only while there is room for another. A wrong value marks its row and names the field's message.

Relation field

Another record as a value: the chosen record as a related-record link, and a Change button that opens a picker dialog. With nothing chosen yet the field reads as unset and the button says Choose. The picker is built from the list components, a filter box and a grid with a radio in each row, and lists only records that can be chosen.

Filter box

A search input that narrows a list as you type: a GET <form class="filter-box" role="search"> with the filter icon and an input type="search". Its placeholder reads "Filter …", never "Search …", and it stops at 360px wide.

Form action bar

The last thing in a form. It sticks to the bottom of the window while a long form scrolls. Actions on the whole record sit at the left as ghost buttons; Cancel and the one primary action sit at the right, in .end. The form at the top of this page ends with one.
