Navigation

Moving between pages and between parts of a page. Every one is made of real links or a GET form, so it works before HTMX is added. The styles are in assets/css/navigation.css.

Side nav

The nav down the left of this page. Each link is an icon and a <span> label; the current page's link carries aria-current="page", and a .nav-label heads a group of links. In the icon rail the labels leave the screen but stay in the accessibility tree, and each link's title gives it a tooltip. data-nav="rail" or "full" on .app overrides the width-based default.

Breadcrumbs

Where the page sits. An ordered list of links ending in the current page, which is text with aria-current="page".

Menu

A native popover of actions, opened by the button that names it in popovertarget. It opens under that button with its right edge on the button's, as a menu at the right end of a row or a header needs. Each item is a link or a button with an icon; an <hr> separates groups, and an action that cannot be undone comes last, in .danger. An icon-only button that opens a menu says whose actions they are in its aria-label.
