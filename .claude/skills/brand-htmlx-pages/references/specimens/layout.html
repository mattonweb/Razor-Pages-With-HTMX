Layout

The frame of every page and the surfaces content sits on. The styles are in assets/css/layout.css.

App shell

This page is the shell: the header across the top, the side nav down the left and the main content beside it. The header holds the menu button and the brand at the left, and at the right whatever belongs to the whole application, ending with the avatar. The nav is full from 900px and an icon rail below that; the menu button switches between the two. A skip link comes first in the page.

Avatar

The signed-in user's initials, at the right end of the header. It is the only avatar: grids, panels and histories show people's names, not avatars. It carries role="img" and an aria-label naming the user.

Page header

Breadcrumbs, then the title row: the title at the left, the page's actions at the right with the one primary action last, then an optional muted line. A page about one record puts the record's status chip, and its version badge if it has one, beside the title in a .title, and says under it who changed the record last and when.

Card

The surface every list, form and panel sits on. A .card-header names what the card holds and may carry one action; .card-body pads the content. A card is a size container, so what is inside it adapts to the card's width rather than the window's. .cards stacks cards with the standard gap between them.

A card with a header

Body text in a card. Paragraph text fills the card's width, however wide the card is.

A card with a body and no header.

Toolbar

The row at the top of a list's card: a filter box at the left and a segmented control at the right end. On a narrow card the two wrap onto separate lines.
